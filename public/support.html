Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an API key?
No. CPU opponents, the free AI opponent and online multiplayer need no account. An API key from Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter is only needed to play against your own choice of AI model; it is stored on your device and sent only to that provider.

The free AI says the daily limit is reached.
The free opponent allows a few games per player per day, and a shared daily allowance for everyone. Both reset at midnight UTC. CPU opponents, Apple Intelligence (on supported devices) and your own API key are not limited.

How do I play with a friend?
Choose Multiplayer on the start screen, then share the room code or the invitation link. Your friend can join from the app or from the website.

Where is my data?
Settings, statistics and API keys stay on your device. Games against an AI opponent send only the current game to that service; see the privacy policy. The Statistics screen has a Clear All button, and a key is removed by clearing its field in Settings.
