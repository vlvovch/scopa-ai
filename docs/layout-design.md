Vision for the layout design for desktop browser. If necessary, ditch the grid layout.

1. Playing area (cards on the table + dealer deck) in the middle of the screen. Playing area space: no more than for 8 cards in one row (less if not enough space, use second row if more cards than fits into one row during playing).
2. Scorboard: top of the screen, horizontally aligned with the left edge of the playing area.
3. Menu buttons: to the top-right of the scoreboard, just like it is now.
4. Opponent (CPU) pile: top of the screen, horizontally aligned with the right edge of the playing area.
5. CPU deck: on top of the playing area with some margin (relative to window/card height). Anchored to playing area only. You should allow it to overlap veritcally with scoreboard/pile if needed.
6. Human deck: on bottom of the playing area with some margin (relative to window/card height). Anchored to playing area only. You should allow it to overlap veritcally with controls/pile if needed.
7. Human pile: bottom of the screen (with healthy margin), horizontally aligned with the left edge of the playing area.
8. Controls (turn, button to play). Bottom of the screen (with healthy margin), horizontally aligned with the right edge of the playing area. 
9. Card size: relative to window height, scales with zoom level
